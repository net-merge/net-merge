Privacy

net-merge has no accounts, analytics or telemetry. Besides passing on your own apps' traffic, it only
connects to:

  - 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 (Cloudflare and Google public DNS), port 443: a connection that sends no data,
    to see whether each of your connections has internet.

  - api.github.com: the tray reads the newest release number, at start and every 6 hours.

  - ipwho.is, or ipinfo.io if that fails: the tray asks who provides each connection, to show the
    provider's name. The service sees that connection's public IP address. This is on by default.
    Untick "Show ISP names" on the next pages, or "Show ISP Names" in the tray menu, to stop it.

Privacy policy and code signing policy: https://net-merge.org/#code-signing
